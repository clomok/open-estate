Questions people ask

The answers a family reaches for first. Where the documents are, who to call, what to do on day one.

Where are the physical documents?

The original trust documents are in the fireproof safe in the master closet. The combination is recorded under the trust's notes on The Trust page.

Who is the primary contact at the bank?

The branch manager at the main local branch. They should have a copy of the Certificate of Trust on file. Bank accounts and their people are under Holdings.

What do we do immediately upon passing?

  1. Record the date of passing on The Trust; the transition playbook is offered from there.
  2. Order ten certified copies of the death certificate.
  3. Call the estate attorney and the financial advisor, both under Contacts.
